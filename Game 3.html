HOW TO PLAY & TRICKS

Controls

Accelerate / Brake: W / S or Up / Down Keys

Lean Back / Forward: A / D or Left / Right Keys

Nitro Boost: SPACEBAR (Requires Nitro Fuel)

Air Stunt Pose: SHIFT (Perform while airborne)

Stunts & Combos

• Backflip / Frontflip: Rotate 360° completely in the air.

• Wheelie: Drive on rear wheel only for >0.5 seconds.

• Big Air: Catch massive hangtime off ramps!

• Superman Stunt: Hold SHIFT in high air for bonus score.

Tip: Land parallel to slopes to avoid crashing and maintain momentum!
